  • Tap result → adds item with last-known qty + price; sheet snaps closed; toast "Hozzáadva ✓".

  • Sheet drag down → dismiss when velocity > 800 px/s or > 30% drag.

05

Bolt mód ★ in-aisle · pure black · 72 pt rows · 56 pt checkboxes

— The hero screen

The whole product comes alive here. Pure black canvas to fight glare, 72 pt rows so a glance from arm's length lands on the right item, and 56 pt fist-sized checkboxes that survive a moving cart and gloves. Checked rows do not reorder — the user's mental map of "where Tej was" stays intact. Screen-on-toggle (moon icon) prevents the auto-lock that ruins flow halfway through the dairy aisle.
